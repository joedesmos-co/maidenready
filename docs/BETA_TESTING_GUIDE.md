# MaidenReady Limited Beta — Tester Guide

Thank you for testing MaidenReady. This is a limited beta for FPV pilots, not a
public launch and not a store.

## What to test

Spend about 15 minutes on the following:

1. **Home** — open the site on desktop and phone.
2. **Starter build** — open Presets, choose one of the six starter builds, and
   load it.
3. **Change parts** — change the frame, motors, props, and battery. Watch the
   drone layout, price, mass, flight estimate, and compatibility update.
4. **Inspect the build** — click a hotspot or category to open the picker. Check
   specs, fit status, and why a part is incompatible.
5. **Breakdown** — open **Cost & mass breakdown**. Check whether the quantities,
   package sizes, included AIO electronics, and totals make sense.
6. **Save and reload** — save the build, refresh the page, and load it again.
7. **Share** — copy the build URL and open it in another tab or on your phone.
8. **Mobile** — use the sticky summary, full-screen picker, and bottom sheet.
9. **Feedback** — use **Beta feedback** in the footer.

## What to report

Use the footer **Beta feedback** form. Useful reports include:

- build class
- device and browser
- what you expected
- what happened
- which part or build was involved
- whether the problem is repeatable

Please do not include passwords or private account details.

## Known limitations

- The catalog has **244 unverified entries, 89 partially verified, and 0 fully
  verified**. Product specs and prices can be incomplete or out of date.
- Several preset battery pack quantities are not verified. The UI warns when a
  price is treated as one pack.
- Some AIO host prices are not verified.
- Radio/transmitter compatibility is not checked.
- Flight time, thrust, top speed, and mass are estimates, not measured data.
- Stock and availability are not tracked.
- Product photography is intentionally not displayed while reuse rights are
  unresolved. Technical illustrations are used instead.
- Starter builds are starting part lists, not physically assembled or tested
  kits.
- Nothing here certifies airworthiness, safety, or physical fit.

## What not to assume

- “No known conflicts” means the static checks found no known conflict. It does
  not mean the build has been physically test-fitted.
- An unverified part is not confirmed compatible.
- An AIO may include an ESC, receiver, or VTX. The breakdown shows when those are
  included and counted once.
- Shipping, taxes, tools, goggles, transmitters, chargers, and spare packs are not
  included in the estimated build price.

## Beta feedback workflow

1. Use **Beta feedback** in the footer.
2. Choose build class, device, and feedback area.
3. Describe the issue and optionally add a reply-to email.
4. Choose **Copy report** and send it to the beta coordinator, or **Email report**
   to open your mail app with a structured report.

No account is created. The form does not upload data to a MaidenReady backend.
